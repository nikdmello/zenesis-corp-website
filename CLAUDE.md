# Zenesis Content Guidance

## Social and newsletter links

- When drafting a LinkedIn post or newsletter from a Zenesis article, always include the direct article link when a published or planned URL is available.
- Use the full canonical `https://www.zenesiscorp.com/...` URL and verify it against the article slug in `src/lib/insights.ts`.
- For LinkedIn, place the article URL in a clear call to action near the end of the post.
- For newsletters, link the article title or the main "Read more" call to action.
- Do not use the homepage or generic Insights page when a direct article URL exists.
