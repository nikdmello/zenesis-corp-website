Q1.Do I need a local partner in my business or can I own it 100%?
If you set up a Local Company licensed by the Department of Economic Development (DED) then you need to have a UAE national partner.  In case of companies with a Trading License, the UAE national partner will own 51% of the company though memorandums are set in place to give full operational powers to expatriate investors.  In case of companies with a Professional License, the expatriate investor will own 100% of the company while the UAE national will only act as a service agent and will no share in the company.

Mainland Companies in Dubai
The most adopted legal structure in mainland UAE is a Limited Liability Company (LLC) and a Branch Office (Branch). In addition, it is also possible to establish a Representative Office (also known as a Liaison Office) which is a legal structure identical to that of a Branch, however its activities are limited to serving as an administrative and marketing center for the parent company.

Top Benefits of Setting Up a Mainland Company:
No minimum capital requirement to form a Mainland Company in Dubai
No restriction on the number of visas
Wide choice of office locations anywhere in Dubai
No currency restrictions
Mainland companies are permitted to take up governmental contracts

Types Of Mainland in Dubai

SOLE PROPRIETORSHIP
Owned and run by one individual who is fully responsible for the business and its liabilities. 


LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY (LLC)
The most common type, providing limited liability for owners. It can have multiple shareholders and is suitable for local and international operations. 


PARTNERSHIP
A business with two or more partners. There are general partnerships (all partners are liable) and limited partnerships (some partners have limited liability). 


CIVIL COMPANY
For professional service providers like lawyers and engineers. Foreign ownership is often allowed, but a local service agent might be required. 


BRANCH FOREIGN COMPANY
Allows foreign companies to have a physical presence and conduct business in the UAE. 


PUBLIC SHAREHOLDING COMPANY(PJSC)
Shares can be traded on the stock market. This is for large corporations. 


PRIVATE SHAREHOLDING COMPANY(PrJSC)
Shares are not publicly traded. This is for smaller corporations that are not public.


JOINT VENTURE
A partnership formed for a specific project, often between local and foreign companies.

Free Zone Companies in Dubai
UAE Free Zones are designed to empower entrepreneurs and global investors with a host of business-friendly benefits. They offer 100% foreign ownership, zero personal and corporate tax in most zones, and a simplified, hassle-free company setup process that allows you to get started quickly.

With state-of-the-art infrastructure, access to duty-free trade, and full repatriation of capital and profits, Freezones create the perfect environment for international businesses to thrive.

Strategically located, they also provide global connectivity through world-class ports, airports, and logistics networks, making them the ideal hub for expansion into the Middle East, Africa, and Asia

Offshore Company Setup
Offshore Companies have been used by people all over the world to hold their property and investments in a safe and secure jurisdiction free of tax. In most jurisdictions, confidentiality is guaranteed, and the details of beneficial owners remain with the registered agent of the company. This is also a very good vehicle for people who do not wish to conduct business locally. Receiving commission payments or for consultancy services rendered offshore is possible. Offshore Companies can have a local bank account provided all due diligence is submitted. The UAE offers Offshore Company set ups in Jebel Ali (part of Dubai), Ras Al Khaimah and Ajman.

Learn More

Ways to set up an Offshore Company in Dubai

JAFZA
JAFZA is the oldest and most respected of the Free Zones that have been allowed to operate in the emirate of Dubai. This type of entity is allowed to trade internationally and is also allowed to own property in Dubai.


RAK OFFSHORE
RAKICC is responsible for the registration and incorporation of International Business Companies, as well as providing a full suite of registry services related to such activities.


Ajman Offshore
Ajman Offshore is a cost-effective UAE company setup option under Ajman Free Zone, designed for international business, asset holding, and tax-efficient operations outside the UAE. It offers 100% foreign ownership, zero corporate tax, and quick setup without the need for a physical office.

Why
Document Attestation is Mandatory in UAE
The UAE government mandates attestation of all foreign documents to ensure authenticity, prevent fraud, and preserve the integrity of its legal system. 

Without proper attestation: 

Your documents will not be accepted by UAE authorities
You cannot use them for work, study, immigration, or legal matters
Applications for residency, employment, education, or business can be rejected

Once your business is registered for Corporate Tax and has a Tax Registration Number (TRN), you must file a return annually with the Federal Tax Authority (FTA). 

🕒 Filing Deadline:
Returns must be submitted within 9 months after the end of your financial year. 

📄 What’s Included in Your Filing:

Total taxable income and allowable expenses
Net profit subject to corporate tax
Applicable tax credits, exemptions, or deductions
Tax payable or refundable amount 
💻 Submission Method:
Returns must be submitted electronically via the FTA portal. 

💰 Payment Terms:
Any tax liability must be paid within the deadline to avoid fines or interest. 

🗃️ Record Keeping:
Maintain detailed records and documents for 5 years to comply with audit regulations.

What Is Corporate Tax &
Who Must Register?
Corporate Tax is a direct tax on the net profit of businesses, introduced to align with international tax standards and promote financial transparency in the UAE. 

 Registration is mandatory for all businesses including those with profits exceeding AED 375,000 annually : 

Mainland companies
Free zone entities (even those with 0% tax incentives)
Foreign company branches
Self-employed individuals and freelancers
Startups and SMEs
Non-residents with UAE-sourced income or a permanent establishment
Even tax-exempt entities may still be required to notify the FTA of their exemption.

Corporate Tax Registration Process
Profit threshold eligibility check
Document collection and validation
Emara Tax portal account setup
Application submission and tracking
Post-registration compliance guidance
🚫 Exemptions from Registration
Some entities may qualify for exemption from corporate tax but may still need to notify the FTA: 
Government or government-controlled entities 
Extractive and non-extractive natural resource businesses 
Approved charities, NGOs, and public benefit organizations 
Qualifying investment and pension funds 
Zenesis assists in filing exemption notifications where applicable.
